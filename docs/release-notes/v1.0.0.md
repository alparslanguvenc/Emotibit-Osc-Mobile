First public release of **EmotiBit Osc Mobile**, an unofficial Android app to start, watch and stop EmotiBit recordings without a laptop.

### Highlights

- Connect to EmotiBit over your phone's hotspot. The app reconnects automatically whenever the device comes back in range.
- Start/stop recordings and answer EmotiBit's time syncs. A safe stop collects fresh syncs before ending, even if you press it while out of range.
- Live plots of EDA, PPG, heart rate, temperature and IMU.
- Sync marker (flash + beep + note in the file) for aligning video.
- English and Turkish, light and dark theme.

### Install

Download the APK below and open it on your phone (Android 10+). Allow installs from your browser or file manager when asked. If Google Play Protect offers to scan the app, choose **Scan app**. See the [README](https://github.com/alparslanguvenc/Emotibit-Osc-Mobile#install) for details and the [user guide](https://github.com/alparslanguvenc/Emotibit-Osc-Mobile/blob/main/docs/USER_GUIDE.md) for the field workflow.

This is an early release. Please run a short pilot recording before an important session, and report problems in [Issues](https://github.com/alparslanguvenc/Emotibit-Osc-Mobile/issues).
